Four identities. Not four blues.

Each one is a different neutral temperature and a different accent family — not a reskin of the same palette. The accent is two tones of one hue: a saturated fill for buttons, and a lighter text tone for numbers and labels. That split is what lets an accent be an actual colour instead of a safe mid-grey. Every value below was generated against the rules, then rejected unless all nine checks passed.
