Apple motion — spring lab

iOS animates on springs, not bezier curves. A cubic-bezier is monotonic, so it physically cannot reproduce the overshoot-and-settle that makes iOS feel alive. These springs are solved from the damped-oscillator equation and emitted as CSS linear() easings — real spring motion that still runs on the compositor.

The curves

Dashed line = the target value. Anything crossing it overshoots and settles — that is the spring, and it is exactly what a bezier cannot do.

Side by side

Watch the trailing edge. The bezier arrives and stops dead; the springs arrive, overshoot a little, and settle. That settle is the entire difference in feel.

Overshoot = how far past the target it travels. Settles = time to stay within 0.1% of target. Stops = number of samples in the emitted linear().
