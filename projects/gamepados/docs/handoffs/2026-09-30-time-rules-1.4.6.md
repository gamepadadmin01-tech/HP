## The rules (owner's answers)
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Free time | 30 minutes **once**, at first launch. No daily time. |
| Cap | Ads offered only while **under 1 hour** is left; one ad still adds the **full 30** (max 89 min). |
| 3-day launch gift | **Removed.** Existing holders keep it until it ends. |
| Existing users | Get the one-time 30 minutes **once** too. |

## Two things found on the way
1. **Signed-in lockout bug (fixed).** `playtime.ts` beginPlay set the cap from
   `today.limitSeconds`. With FREE at 0 every signed-in free player would have been blocked before
   the first heartbeat. The cap is now `usedSeconds + remainingSeconds` (remaining includes the bank).
2. **Privacy policy was wrong since the ads went in.** It said "the apps show no ads and contain no
   advertising SDKs". Rewritten with an "Ads in the app" section (AdMob, advertising ID, consent,
   the signed-in reward callback). Play and AdMob both require this — it must be live before the
   Play upload.
