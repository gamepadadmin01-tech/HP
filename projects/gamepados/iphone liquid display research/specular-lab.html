Gyro specular highlight

The highlight is a soft blob clipped to the rim. Tilting the phone translates it — a compositor-only transform, so it costs nothing per frame. Refraction stays static and cached; only this moves.

Tuning

Apple's stated ceiling is 6px. Past ~10px it stops reading as a highlight and starts reading as a moving object — and it is a nausea trigger for motion-sensitive users.

Raw gyro is noisy. This is a one-euro-style low pass; too little and the highlight jitters while the phone rests on a table.
