# GamepadOS — 75-Second YouTube Reveal — Production Kit

**Format:** Landscape 16:9 · 1920×1080 (or 4K) · cinematic announcement/reveal · "available now" energy.
**One idea:** *The controller you already own is in your pocket.*
**Shoot every clip ~2× longer than needed.** Land the logo + CTA hard at the end.

---

## PART 1 | The 75-Second Sequence

### 0:00–0:06 — COLD OPEN (Tension)
- **Visual:** Slow push-in on a glowing PC monitor mid-game, a controller-shaped empty space on the desk. No music, just ambient game audio + room tone.
- **On-screen:** *(none yet — let it breathe)*
- **Edit:** Dark, moody, single key light on the monitor. Build quiet tension.

### 0:06–0:12 — THE PROBLEM
- **Visual:** Hands fumbling on keyboard mid-game, awkward, a near-miss/crash.
- **On-screen:** "PC gaming deserves a controller."
- **Edit:** Hold on the frustration beat. Record-scratch into 0.5s silence.

### 0:12–0:18 — THE TURN
- **Visual:** Hand leaves keyboard, picks up the phone. Phone already glowing with the controller UI.
- **On-screen:** "You already own one."
- **Edit:** Whip-pan / speed-ramp keyboard → phone. **MUSIC DROPS** — the beat starts here.

### 0:18–0:30 — THE REVEAL (Hero Sequence)
- **Visual:** Rapid intercut: thumbs on phone ↔ PC game reacting instantly. 4–6 hard cuts proving sync.
- **On-screen:** "Your phone is the controller." then pulse "ZERO LAG"
- **Edit:** Snappiest section. Each cut lands on a beat. This sells the whole product.

### 0:30–0:38 — SETUP IS NOTHING
- **Visual:** PC shows QR code → phone scans → "Connected ✓". Three clean beats.
- **On-screen:** "Scan. Connect. Play. — under 10 seconds."
- **Edit:** Speed-ramp the scan, slow-mo on the green tick. Click/chime SFX.

### 0:38–0:54 — FEATURE MONTAGE (the "wow" reel)
Four quick feature beats, ~4s each, music-driven:
1. **Gyro:** tilt phone, on-screen car/crosshair follows → "Real gyro aiming."
2. **Customization:** drag/resize/recolor buttons; 2–3 layouts flash → "Build any layout."
3. **Haptics:** big in-game hit, phone visibly buzzes → "Feel every hit."
4. **Layouts:** racing / FPS / D-pad presets flash by → "Made for every game."
- **Edit:** One beat per feature, kinetic text, motion blur on transitions. Keep it MOVING.

### 0:54–1:02 — THE VALUE PUNCH
- **Visual:** Expensive controller on desk nudged aside; phone takes its place. Apple-style beauty shot.
- **On-screen:** "$60 controller → Free to try"
- **Edit:** Strike-through on the price. Swoosh SFX. Clean, minimal, single accent light.

### 1:02–1:10 — HERO BEAUTY SHOT
- **Visual:** Phone in a stand in front of the glowing monitor, both screens running, slow push-in, nobody touching it.
- **On-screen:** *(begin fading logo in)*
- **Edit:** Slow dolly/push-in, light sweep, subtle slow-mo.

### 1:10–1:15 — LOGO + CTA (End Card)
- **Visual:** Full-screen graphic. Logo snaps to center on the final beat.
- **On-screen:** "Your phone. Your controller. Zero lag." / "Available now → gamepad.space"
- **Edit:** Music hard-stops (no fade). Hold 3s. Add Subscribe + link nudge for YouTube.

## PART 3 | Director's Notes (YouTube-specific)

- **Hook in 6 seconds.** YouTube retention lives or dies in the first 6s. The cold-open tension → problem must grab fast.
- **Landscape framing.** Leave headroom; keep action centered (works in small embeds + thumbnails).
- **Thumbnail = phone + monitor in one frame** with bold "ZERO LAG" text. Shoot a dedicated still for this.
- **Caption everything.** Burn in text — many watch muted. High contrast, inside the title-safe zone.
- **One accent color** across all overlays = matches the app UI = looks like a real brand.
- **End screen.** Leave the last 5–10s graphic-friendly so YouTube end-screen cards (Subscribe, link) fit.
- **Export:** 1080p (or 4K) H.264, ~16 Mbps, 24 or 30 fps. I can auto-render this with FFmpeg.
- **Repurpose:** the 0:18–0:30 reveal + 1:10 end card = your TikTok/Reels vertical cut. One shoot, two formats.
