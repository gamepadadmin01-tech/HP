# Brief: Google Play preview video for GamepadOS 2.0 (for Emergent)

## What to make
A 30-second promotional preview video for the Google Play listing of **GamepadOS**, an Android app that turns a phone
into a low-latency game controller (gamepad) for a Windows PC, over Wi-Fi or a USB cable.

Goal: in the first 5 seconds a viewer must understand "my phone becomes a controller for my PC games".

## Technical requirements (Google Play rules — must follow)
- Length: 30 seconds (25–35 s is fine).
- **Landscape 16:9.** Master in **3840×2160 (4K) at 60 fps**; smooth motion, no dropped frames.
- Delivered as an MP4 (H.264 or H.265), plus a 1920×1080 60 fps copy.
- It will be uploaded to YouTube as **Public or Unlisted**, embeddable, not age-restricted, and **with no ads**.
- **Music must be royalty-free with NO Content ID** (no YouTube copyright claims). A claim makes Google hide the video.
  Prefer an energetic electronic / synthwave track at ~120–128 BPM, or no music. Cuts should land on the beat.
- No voice-over needed. All text on screen, short and large (readable on a phone screen at small size).

## Must NOT appear
- **No prices** or currency amounts anywhere.
- **No other companies' names or logos**: no Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, Steam logos, no real game titles or game
  footage. Use generic, made-up game visuals (an abstract racing track, a neon arena) if a game is shown.
- No "best", "#1", "free forever", or claims we cannot prove. No ranking or award badges.
- Do not show people's faces.

## Brand
- App name: **GamepadOS** (store title: "Gamepad: PC Phone Controller").
- Logo: an orange square app icon with a near-black "G" glyph (file: `play-icon-512.png`, provided).
- Colours: background warm dark brown **#1A1210** (darker #140D0B), accent orange **#FF5500**, text off-white **#F6EDE9**,
  muted text **#A89A93**, connected-green **#7BD88F** (use sparingly).
- Fonts: headlines **Space Grotesk** (bold), body **Inter**.
- Feel: calm, premium, dark, one orange accent. Motion: smooth springy ease-outs, one thing moving at a time, text
  stays still long enough to read (at least 1.5 s per line). No metronomic, mechanical pacing.

## Storyboard (30 s)
| Time | Scene | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Dark brown background. A phone held sideways glides in; the controller screen lights up. Small orange glow. | **Your phone is now a PC gamepad** |
| 3–7 s | The phone sits in front of a stylised PC monitor showing an abstract neon racing game; a thumb press on the phone makes the car react on the monitor (show cause → effect). | **Play PC games with your phone** |
| 7–11 s | Split: a Wi-Fi symbol and a USB cable, each connecting phone ↔ PC. Connect screen shown. | **Wi-Fi or USB. Low latency.** |
| 11–15 s | Scan a QR code on the PC screen with the phone; a green "Connected" pill appears. | **Scan the code. Start playing.** |
| 15–19 s | Phone tilts left/right like a steering wheel; the car on the monitor turns with it. | **Tilt to steer** |
| 19–23 s | Layout editor: buttons get dragged and resized. | **Build your own layout** |
| 23–26 s | Four phones side by side, each labelled P1, P2, P3, P4, all linked to one PC. | **Up to 4 players on one PC** |
| 26–30 s | End card: app icon centred, name below, short tagline, "Get it on Google Play" style text (plain text, NOT the official Google badge artwork). | **GamepadOS** / *Turn your phone into a PC gamepad* |
