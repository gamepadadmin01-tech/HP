## Full description (4000 max)

Turn your phone into a PC gamepad. GamepadOS makes your Android phone a low-latency game controller for your Windows PC, over Wi-Fi or a USB cable. No dongle, no extra hardware: your PC sees a standard game controller, so games that support a gamepad just work.

★ HOW IT WORKS
1. Install the free Gamepad Server on your Windows 10 or 11 PC from gamepad.space.
2. Open GamepadOS on your phone and scan the QR code, or connect with a USB cable.
3. Play. Your phone is now a wireless or wired PC gamepad.

★ LOW LATENCY, WI-FI OR USB
• USB mode for the lowest latency, as low as a few milliseconds.
• Wi-Fi mode for freedom from cables. 5 GHz Wi-Fi gives the best response.
• Stable connection with clear help: if your PC does not answer, tap "See why?" for the likely causes, in order.

★ A REAL PHONE CONTROLLER
• Analog sticks, triggers, bumpers, D-pad and face buttons, laid out like a console gamepad.
• Gyro steering: tilt your phone to steer in racing games.
• Touchpad for camera and mouse-style control.
• Strong, crisp vibration on every press.
• Smooth on 90 and 120 Hz screens.
• Built-in layouts: Standard and Standard Racing.

★ CUSTOM LAYOUTS
• Build your own controller layout in the editor: move, resize and add buttons, sticks and the touchpad.
• Tune right stick sensitivity for faster camera turns or finer aim.
• Back up your layouts with a free account and get them back on a new phone.

★ PLAY TOGETHER
Connect up to four phones to one PC. Each phone is its own gamepad, Player 1 to 4, for local multiplayer and couch co-op games.

★ FREE TO START
GamepadOS is free to download, and every player gets free play time to start, no account needed. Watch a short ad for more time whenever you like, or get Lifetime for unlimited play, paid once through Google Play. Ads appear only when you tap to watch one.

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your controller input goes straight from your phone to your PC over your own network or cable. It never passes through our servers.

★ WORKS WITH
• Windows 10 and Windows 11 PCs with the free Gamepad Server.
• Emulators and any PC game that supports a game controller.
• Racing, action, sports, fighting and platform games.
