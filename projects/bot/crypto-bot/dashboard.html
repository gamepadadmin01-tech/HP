Emergency controls

In the bot's folder, create an empty file named STOP to block new trades (open stop-losses stay on the exchange), or FLATTEN to sell everything and stop. Delete STOP to resume. Closing the bot window also stops new trades; stop-loss orders already placed stay active on Binance.
