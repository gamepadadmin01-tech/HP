# GamepadOS — Instagram Reel Kit ("The Connect Cut")

**Format:** Vertical 9:16 · 1080×1920 · 26–30 seconds · Instagram Reels.
**Goal:** Emotional connection, not an ad. People share what they *feel*, and they feel stories about people — not features.
**One idea:** *The controller you couldn't afford was in your pocket the whole time.*

This reuses the footage from the 30-second Ad Production Kit (same Clip IDs), plus **3 new "human" shots** (Bucket E). The old kits have zero face shots — for connection, your face IS the product shot.

---

## PART 1 | The 28-Second Sequence

### 0:00–0:02 — THE HOOK (face + pain, first frame matters)
- **Visual:** Your face, lit by the monitor, scrolling a shopping page showing a controller priced ₹4,000–6,000. Small sigh / close the tab. *(NEW clip E1)*
- **On-screen (snaps in frame 1):** "Couldn't afford a controller for my PC…"
- **Why it works:** Reels are decided in 1.5 seconds. A face + a money problem = instantly relatable. No logo, no branding yet.

### 0:02–0:05 — THE STRUGGLE (make them nod)
- **Visual:** Keyboard play, awkward, ends in a crash/near-miss. *(reuse A1_keyboard_struggle)*
- **On-screen:** "so this was my life"
- **Edit:** Let the fail land. A tiny beat of humor here earns the next 20 seconds.

### 0:05–0:08 — THE TURN
- **Visual:** Hand leaves keyboard, picks up the phone — controller UI already glowing. *(reuse C1_grab_phone)*
- **On-screen:** "then I turned my phone into one."
- **Edit:** Music drops HERE. Whip-pan / speed-ramp keyboard → phone.

### 0:08–0:14 — THE PROOF (money shot)
- **Visual:** Two-thumb gameplay intercut with the PC game reacting instantly — 4 hard cuts in ~3s. *(reuse B1_thumbs_playing + A2_game_reacting)*
- **On-screen:** pulse "ZERO LAG" on each cut.
- **Edit:** Snappiest section. Every cut on a beat. This is what makes the story believed, not just liked.

### 0:14–0:18 — THE MAGIC (two quick beats, no lingering)
- **Beat 1:** QR on monitor → phone scans → "Connected ✓". *(A4 + B2)* — text: "connects in 10 seconds"
- **Beat 2:** Tilt the phone, car/crosshair follows. *(B3_gyro_tilt)* — text: "even real gyro aiming"
- **Edit:** 2 seconds each, chime SFX on the tick, motion blur on the tilt.

### 0:18–0:23 — THE PAYOFF (this is the "connect" moment)
- **Visual:** You leaning back in the chair, relaxed, genuinely enjoying the game — a real smile, maybe a small fist-pump on a win. Shoot it until one take feels TRUE, not acted. *(NEW clip E2)*
- **On-screen:** "₹0. And it's mine."
- **Optional:** hand a second phone to a friend who joins — two-player beat. *(NEW clip E3)*
- **Edit:** Slow down 10–15%. Let the music open up. This frame is your cover image candidate.

### 0:23–0:26 — HERO SHOT
- **Visual:** Phone in a stand in front of the glowing monitor, both screens alive, slow push-in. *(reuse C4_hero_pushin)*
- **On-screen:** logo fades in.

### 0:26–0:28 — END CARD
- **On-screen:** "GamepadOS — your phone is the controller." / "Free → link in bio"
- **Edit:** Music hard-stop. Hold 2s. Keep the card INSIDE the safe zone (see Part 3).

## PART 3 | Instagram-Specific Director's Notes

- **Shoot everything vertical (9:16) this time.** Monitor at the top of frame, phone/hands at the bottom — Reels framing, not TV framing.
- **Safe zones:** keep all text out of the bottom ~350px (caption/UI overlays it) and the right ~120px (like/share buttons). Center your text cards.
- **Audio:** pick a **trending audio** in the Reels editor, duck it to ~20% under your own SFX/music. Reels with trending audio get pushed harder. Keep the record-scratch + chime + thump SFX from the ad kit.
- **Burn in every caption.** Most people watch muted. ≤5 words per card, high contrast, one accent color matching the app UI.
- **Cover frame:** use the E2 smile frame with the phone visible — not the logo. Faces stop thumbs.
- **Caption (paste-ready):**
  > Couldn't afford a controller… so I turned my phone into one. Zero lag, real gyro, free to try 🎮 Link in bio.
- **Hashtags (5–8 is plenty):** #pcgaming #gamingsetup #budgetgaming #indiangamer #gamingindia #gamepad #pcgamer
- **First comment:** pin the link (gamepad.space) — Reels captions don't have tappable links.
- **Post timing:** evenings 7–10pm IST, weekdays. Reply to every comment in the first hour — the algorithm watches early engagement.
- **One shoot, three posts:** this 28s connect cut → also trim a 12s version (0:00–0:08 + 0:18–0:28) → also post the E2/E3 moment alone as a casual "day in the life" story.
