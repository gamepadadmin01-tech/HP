Windows 11 blocked it? Read this first

On a fresh Windows 11 install you may see "Smart App Control blocked an app that may be unsafe". That is Windows refusing to run software from a publisher it can't verify — we haven't bought a code-signing certificate yet. It is not a virus warning and there is nothing wrong with the download.

You can check the file yourself before trusting us: right-click the installer → Properties → Digital Signatures, and compare the SHA-256 we publish on the support page.

Your options, honestly: wait until we're signed (it's on our list), or turn Smart App Control off in Windows Security → App & browser control → Smart App Control. Turning it off is permanent — Windows can only switch it back on by resetting the PC, and it protects everything on your machine, not just this app. Please don't do it on our account unless you're genuinely comfortable with that.
